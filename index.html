Backend and calculation

Source data

AMFI publishes the official NAV. Fund houses publish monthly holdings and AUM. Replay supplies synthetic stock prices.

Node.js backend

GET /api/sources?fund=… loads and caches official NAV and holdings. This export bundles dated snapshots of those sources.

JavaScript estimator

The browser matches quotes to holdings by ISIN or symbol and uses the close aligned to the official NAV date.

Official NAV × (1 + sum of weighted stock returns)—

Weights without a matching price contribute zero to the estimate. Portfolio coverage shows the share of NAV priced by the model.

The model covers Indian-listed stocks matched to quotes. Monthly disclosures can lag portfolio changes. Overseas holdings, cash, derivatives, fees, dividends and corporate actions can also change the final NAV.
